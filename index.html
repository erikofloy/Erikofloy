About Merrill

Family-owned, Iowa-made, since 1949

Merrill started in a one-car garage in Des Moines, when Noel Merrill Anderson invented the Any Flow® Yard Hydrant. The company moved to Storm Lake, Iowa in 1957 and is still there today, now in its third generation of family ownership. We're best known for frost-proof yard hydrants, but we carry a full line of water well products, so you can cover a lot of ground with one supplier.
